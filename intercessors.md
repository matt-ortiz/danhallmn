*Mission & How to Join

State Intercessors is a network of people of faith—pastors, ministers, and believers across Minnesota—committed to standing in the gap through prayer for our state’s leaders, our nation, and the world.

Prayer changes things. When faithful people intercede for those in positions of authority—in government, education, law enforcement, and public service—they are participating in something Scripture calls us to do: “I urge you, first of all, to pray for all people and to give thanks for them” (1 Timothy 2:1-2).

The State Intercessors network exists to coordinate that intercession. We gather prayer requests from leaders and citizens. We share prayer focus areas weekly. We believe that when we pray for those who lead, we are participating in God’s redemptive work in our state and nation.

Join us. Sign up below to receive weekly prayer focus areas and requests. Whether you have 5 minutes or an hour to pray each week, your intercession matters. You will receive email updates on:

Prayer requests from Minnesota’s leaders
Current focus areas for state, national, and world issues
Encouragement and Scripture for your prayer life

“To increase Him in us”—that’s our guiding motto. When we pray, we invite Christ to increase His influence in our leaders’ hearts and in the decisions that shape our state.*
