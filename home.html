About Dan

A voice you can trust.

Dan Hall has spent more than forty years in ministry, education, and politics — ten of them in the Minnesota Senate. He has served as a chaplain to metro police departments and founded Midwest Chaplains, which built chaplaincy programs into police and fire departments across the state.

Today he serves as a political chaplain and advisor to Minnesota's leaders, offering spiritual guidance and practical wisdom to the people carrying the weight of public decisions.
