**The Truth Report**

Where faith and public life meet. Where biblical truth informs civic responsibility.

The Truth Report is Dan’s weekly email—written from a vantage point few hold. As a retired Minnesota Senator, evangelical minister, and chaplain to the state’s leaders, Dan brings a unique perspective to the intersection of faith, politics, and culture.

Each week, The Truth Report delivers:

Insight on faith and public life — Articles exploring how biblical truth applies to the decisions shaping our state and nation.

Prayer requests from Minnesota’s leaders — Names and specific needs from those in positions of authority, inviting you to intercede.

On-the-ground reporting — What’s actually happening in Minnesota politics and culture, interpreted through a faith lens.

A community of **10,000 believers** — Pastors, ministers, intercessors, and citizens who refuse to compartmentalize their faith from their civic life.

The Truth Report doesn’t shy away from hard topics. It doesn’t pretend that politics is separate from faith, or that biblical principles don’t apply to public policy. Instead, it models what it means to think Christianly about the world, to pray for those in authority, and to speak truth in a polarized age.

If you care about your faith and your nation’s future, you belong here.
