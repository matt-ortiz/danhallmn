The Truth Report is Dan’s weekly email, written from a perspective few hold: a retired Minnesota Senator now serving as chaplain to the state’s leaders.

Each issue weaves together articles on politics and religion, prayer requests for Minnesota, and reporting on events shaping the state. It reaches **10,000 people of faith** — pastors, ministers, and intercessors — who understand that biblical truth and civic responsibility cannot be kept apart.
