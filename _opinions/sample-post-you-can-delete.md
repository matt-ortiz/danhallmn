This post exists so Dan can see what a published opinion piece looks like on the
site. It was created through the editor, exactly the way every future post will
be. Delete it any time.

## Headings work

Type a heading, and it renders like the one above. Below it, ordinary
paragraphs. **Bold** and *italic* work the way you'd expect, and so do
[links](/about/).

> Block quotes are useful for Scripture or for quoting someone you're
> responding to.

- Bulleted lists
- for laying out points
- one after another

That is the whole format. Write, choose a category, click Publish.
