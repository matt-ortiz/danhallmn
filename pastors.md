You stand in one of the most important positions in our nation. As a pastor, you shepherd people through their deepest questions—about faith, about right and wrong, about how to live biblically in a culture that increasingly challenges biblical truth. That responsibility is both sacred and lonely.

I’ve spent forty years at the intersection of faith and public life—as a minister, a senator, and now as a chaplain to our state’s leaders. I’ve seen what happens when pastors are equipped with both spiritual wisdom and practical insight into the cultural and political currents shaping their congregations. And I’ve seen what happens when they’re not.

This list exists to stand with you. Not to replace your study or your prayer, but to complement it—to give you perspective, encouragement, and resources as you lead your congregation through this pivotal hour in our nation’s history.

You are not alone. Your work matters. And the Gospel you preach is the answer our nation desperately needs.

## What you'll receive

**Shepherding in a Polarized Time**
Practical wisdom for leading congregations through cultural division without compromising biblical truth.

**Faith & Freedom**
Perspective on religious liberty, why it matters, and how to help your congregation understand its importance.

**Prayer & Spiritual Leadership**
Encouragement and Scripture-based insight for your own spiritual walk and for interceding for your congregation and nation.

**Biblical Truth in Public Life**
How to help your people think Christianly about politics, policy, and cultural issues—without being partisan.

**Leadership Reflections**
Lessons from my years in ministry and government about leading with integrity, courage, and conviction.

**News & Insight**
Weekly analysis of what’s happening in Minnesota and nationally from a faith perspective.
